How it works

Brace yourself for the complexity.

1

Press the hotkey

Tap your shortcut from anywhere. A little waveform pops up. Dict is listening.

2

Talk

Say whatever. Ramble. Use filler words. Switch to Portuguese mid-sentence if the mood strikes. Dict keeps up.

3

Text appears

Clean text lands where your cursor was. That is the whole thing. There is no step four.

Privacy

We do not want your data

No tracking. No analytics. No mystery network calls. Dict runs fully offline by default. Cloud cleanup exists if you want it, it is opt-in, and you can ignore it for the rest of your life.

  • Local Whisper via whisper.cpp
  • Runs on macOS, Linux, and Windows
  • Bring your own LLM with Ollama or LM Studio
  • Cloud cleanup is opt-in, never on by default
